The ISC-GEM catalogue and the adaptations described below are distributed under [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). This notice covers ISC-GEM data and adaptations. The research code is separate.

- `isc-gem.zip` preserves the downloaded archive byte for byte, including the main and supplementary CSVs, the KMZ file, and the source notices.
- `isc_gem_snapshot.json` is an adaptation that normalizes selected catalogue fields, records the study events used for threshold counts, and aggregates main and supplementary counts by UTC year. It keeps the two sources distinct and provides a combined sensitivity series.
- The ISC-GEM portions of the time-series results, prediction export, event audit, and derived tables and figures apply study-period and magnitude filters, aggregate forecasts and losses, or record event links between catalogues.
